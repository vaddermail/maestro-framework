# Decision Engine

How the framework makes technical decisions **without fixed opinions and without reopening
everything forever**: options generated by specialists, compared against explicit criteria, decided
by an arbiter (or by the user), recorded in an ADR — and, once validated, **closed**.

## Decision types

| Type | Examples | Who decides | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product/scope** | what goes into the MVP, priorities, target personas | User (with a recommendation) | Phase artifact + `STATE.md` |
| **Structural** | architectural style, stack, cloud, identity model, data strategy | Arbiter proposes → user validates | **ADR required** |
| **Local technical** | date library, module name, log format | Owning agent, within conventions | Note in the artifact/code |
| **Emergency** | mitigating a production incident | Runbook + human in W11 | Post-mortem |

Golden rule: **the more expensive the reversal, the more formal the decision.** A one-day decision
does not deserve an ADR; a three-month one cannot live in a code comment.

## The process for structural decisions

1. **Frame** — the Orchestrator defines the decision question and the **weighted criteria**,
   derived from the artifacts (requirements, NFRs, risks, costs, team). Typical criteria:
   functional fit, total cost (build + operation), operational complexity, team competence,
   reversibility, maturity/stability (`knowledge/permanent-rules.md` §versões estáveis), lock-in.
2. **Propose as a panel** — 2–4 relevant specialists (e.g. `agents/02-architecture/`) produce
   **independent, blind** proposals, each with: a design, honest pros/cons against the criteria,
   costs, risks and a reversal path. A specialist who concludes "my approach does not fit here"
   says so — that is a valid and valuable proposal.
3. **Arbitrate** — the arbiter (e.g. `agents/02-architecture/architecture-arbiter.md`) compares
   against the criteria, may merge ideas, and writes the reasoned decision. The arbiter is
   **never** one of the proposers.
4. **Validate with the user** — in plain language: what was chosen, what was rejected and why,
   what it costs, how it can be reversed. Only then does the ADR become `approved`.
5. **Record** — ADR in `product/02-architecture/decisions/ADR-nnn-title.md`
   (template: `templates/project/ADR-DECISION.md.template`).

## ADR — what it must contain

- **Context:** the problem and the forces at play (the requirements/NFRs applying pressure).
- **Options considered:** all panel proposals, with the essence of the pros/cons — the rejected
  ones stay recorded so nobody re-proposes them without novelty.
- **Decision:** what was chosen, with the criteria that weighed.
- **Consequences:** what gets easier, what gets harder, debts taken on.
- **Reversal:** what changing would cost; warning signs that would justify revisiting.
- **Status:** proposed → approved → (eventually) superseded by ADR-nnn.

## Closed decisions

A decision validated by the user becomes **closed**: agents do not reopen it on their own
initiative. The list of closed decisions lives in the project's `CLAUDE.md` (section
"Decisões fechadas") — it is among the first things any session reads.

Reopening requires **material novelty** (a new requirement, a proven failure, an external change)
and happens in the open: "this contradicts closed decision X, made because Y — do you really want
to reopen it?" If the user asks for something that collides with a closed decision, **warn before
executing** (`MANIFESTO.md` §8). If reopened, the old ADR gets marked `superseded by ADR-nnn` —
it is never deleted.

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Deciding by fashion ("everyone uses X") → ✅ decide by the project's weighted criteria.
- ❌ Facade panel (specialists validating an already-made choice) → ✅ blind proposals,
  independent arbiter.
- ❌ A 10-page ADR-novel → ✅ one dense page; the detail lives in the linked artifacts.
- ❌ Re-litigating closed decisions every session → ✅ closed stays closed until there is novelty.
- ❌ Hiding the "do nothing" option → ✅ the status quo is always one of the options evaluated.
- ❌ Bleeding-edge technology out of enthusiasm → ✅ stable and boring by default; innovation
  where it is a differentiator, with the reason recorded.
